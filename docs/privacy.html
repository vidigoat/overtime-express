Privacy Policy

Overtime Express · Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version

Overtime Express does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (Tocks, stars, gear you have bought and which jobs you have finished) is saved only on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

Children

Because we collect no data at all, the game is safe for players of every age.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if we add optional features that need data, we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before the change ships.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on the project page.
