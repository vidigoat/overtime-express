Overtime Express Support

You own a tiny delivery company. Race the clock across neon rooftops, deliver every parcel, and save up for the bike.

How to play

  • Swipe left or right to change lanes.
  • Swipe up to jump. Swipe up again in the air to dash.
  • Swipe down to slide under beams.
  • Hold your thumb still to slow time (it costs clock).
  • Chain moves to earn time back. Run out and you're in overtime: one more hit ends the run.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app resets it.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue and we'll take a look.
