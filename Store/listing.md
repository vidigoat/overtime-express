## Description
You just started a delivery company. Staff: you. Vehicles: none. You run the rooftops.

OVERTIME EXPRESS is a one-thumb rooftop runner where the clock is your life, your fuel and your paycheck. Swipe to change lanes, jump gaps, slide under beams, grind rails and run along walls. Chain moves together to earn time back, and run out and you're in overtime: one more crash and the parcel's gone.

EVERY JOB IS A STORY
• Deliver a birthday burger to a seven-year-old, a crate of steak to a gym, a royal dinner on fine china, a bubbling witch's stew, and a box that clucks.
• Each parcel changes how you run: heavy ones smash through hurdles, fragile ones crack on hard landings, hot ones melt your clock faster.
• Dot, your dispatcher, keeps you on track. And then there's the ticking parcel nobody sent.

GROW YOUR COMPANY
• Earn wages in Tocks, the city's currency, for speed, care and every Tock you grab on the way.
• Climb from Solo Courier to Overtime Tycoon across six neon districts.
• Buy gear that changes how you play: grip gloves, spring soles, a Tock magnet, bubble wrap, a skateboard, and finally the bike.

GRAB THE COURIER'S LUNCH
• Burger Rush smashes through everything, Soda Fizz doubles every move, Bubble Wrap saves you from a crash and the Tock Magnet pulls money your way.

BUILT FOR ONE THUMB
• Swipe controls tuned for phones, with forgiving timing so near misses feel like skill.
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Just run.
