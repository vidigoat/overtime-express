# App Review screen recording: what to film

Apple wants one continuous recording on a real iPhone (latest iOS) that starts at launch.

## Setup (once)
1. iPhone: install **TestFlight** from the App Store.
2. Open the TestFlight invite email for Overtime Express and tap **View in TestFlight**, then **Install**.
3. Add Screen Recording to Control Center if it isn't there: Settings > Control Center.
4. Fully close Overtime if it's open (swipe it away in the app switcher).

## Record (about 2-3 minutes)
1. Start screen recording from Control Center, then go to the Home Screen.
2. Tap the **Overtime** icon to launch the app. (The video must start with the launch.)
3. Title screen: tap **CLOCK IN**.
4. HQ: pause 2 seconds, then tap **CITY ROUTE**. Scroll the map a little, then go back.
5. Tap **GEAR SHOP**, look at the items for a couple of seconds, then go back.
6. Tap **DELIVER**. Briefing ticket: pause 2 seconds, tap **START SHIFT**.
7. Play the run: follow the tutorial prompts (swipe left/right, up to jump, up again to dash, down to slide) until the parcel is **DELIVERED**.
8. Payout screen: let the numbers finish counting, then tap **BACK TO HQ**.
9. Stop the recording (red pill at the top > Stop).

## Send it back
AirDrop the video from Photos to the Mac (it lands in Downloads). Claude attaches it to the submission.
